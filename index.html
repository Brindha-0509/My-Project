About Our Café

Brewing Pure Passion in Every Single Cup

Founded with a deep love for authentic coffee culture, Brew & Bean is a warm neighborhood sanctuary where craft meets community.

We partner directly with sustainable, high-altitude farms across Colombia, Ethiopia, and Guatemala. Every batch of specialty beans is roasted with precision in-house to unlock rich, complex tasting notes—from velvety chocolate to bright citrus florals.

Whether you need a calm corner to read, a quiet spot to focus, or an uplifting morning espresso with friendly faces, our doors are always open for you.
